######## Pins (Servo/ESC) ######
'''
We are using Raspi 4 model B, change your pins corresponding to your raspi
or modify directely in the scripts of pwm_stearing.py and/or esc.py in the utils
s : signal, g: ground, v: voltage

Servo --> pin s:11(GPIO 17) , pin g:14 , pin v:2
ESC   --> pin s:3(GPIO 2) , pin g:6 , pin v: non
'''
